How it works

Intent in. Safe transfer out.

Every payment flows through a structured pipeline — intent parsing, route validation, rule enforcement, and human approval — before execution begins.

01
Intent parsing
Type what you want to do in plain language. The agent extracts amount, asset, source chain, destination chain, and recipient address into structured JSON.
AI layer
02
Route & rule check
The route checker confirms Injective → Solana CCTP support. The deterministic rule engine approves, denies, or flags the payment against your spending limits.
Rule engine
03
Gas credit logic
OmnisRouter checks your 20 monthly sponsored gas credits. If credits remain, gas is handled automatically. If exhausted, you choose fee deduction or top-up.
Gas layer
04
Human approval
You see the full payment summary — route, amount, fee, rule result — and approve before any transaction is submitted. No autonomous spending without your sign-off.
Trust gate
05
CCTP execution
Upon approval, USDC is burned on Injective, Circle produces the attestation, and the same amount is minted as native USDC to your recipient's Solana address.
CCTP rail
06
Receipt & audit log
Every step is logged — intent parsed, rules validated, approval recorded, attestation confirmed, minting complete. A full receipt with TX hashes is generated for your records.
Trust layer

Product features

Everything a payment needs.

OmnisRouter is built around five modules: intent parsing, route checking, spending rule enforcement, gas credit logic, and payment execution with a full audit log.

Deterministic spending rules
Set max transfer amounts, daily caps, approval thresholds, allowed destination chains, blocked addresses, and an emergency pause. Rules run in code — not AI guesswork.
Sponsored gas credits
20 sponsored gas-credit transfers per month. When credits remain, transfers proceed without you sourcing INJ or SOL. When exhausted, choose fee deduction or top-up.
CCTP route transparency
Plain-English route explanation with a technical details dropdown. You always know the transfer path — Injective burn → Circle attestation → Solana mint — before approving.
Full payment audit log
Every step from intent parsed to minting confirmed is logged with timestamps and metadata. TX hashes link to explorers. Receipts show who paid the fee — always.
